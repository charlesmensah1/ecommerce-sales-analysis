# ecommerce-sales-analysis
**Analysis of e-commerce sales to understand performance, identify trends and support business decisions.**
Vecta Store is an e-commerce company that specialize in selling office stationery, furniture, electronics, apparel and accessories. In the competitive landscape of e-commerce, understanding customer purchasing patterns and the impact of operational factors on sales performance is essential for improving business outcomes. With data on sales transactions, customer demographics, order details, discounts, and payment and shipping preferences, the company can leverage this information to create a more optimized shopping experience, leading to better sales and customer retention.
SCOPE OF ANALYSIS

Below is my major scope of the analysis.

_Sales Trends and Performance_: Analyzing sales volumes and revenue across product categories, seasonal variations, and customer demographics.

_Discount and Payment Methods Impact_: Evaluating how various discount strategies and payment options influence customer purchase decisions and overall sales.

_Shipping and Order Fulfillment Efficiency_: Understanding the role of shipment providers, delivery times, and return handling on customer satisfaction and repeat purchases.

_Customer Purchase Behavior_: Examining purchasing patterns based on customer profiles (age, location, spending habits) to identify high-value customer segments and target them with tailored marketing.

**MY ROLE**

For this project, I conducted a detailed diagnostic analysis of Vecta Store’s online sales data to identify key trends and insights that could drive business growth and operational efficiency. Vecta Store, specializing in office stationery, electronics, and furniture aimed to improve sales performance and customer engagement through data-driven decision-making.

In this analysis, I cleaned and organized a comprehensive dataset, then created a dynamic dashboard with pivot tables and slicers to segment customer purchase behaviour by region, payment method, and sales channel. Key metrics such as average purchase amount, customer demographics, shipping trends, and discount impact were calculated to identify patterns in purchasing behaviours. I also performed a comparative sales analysis across different periods, clearly showing growth trends.

This project demonstrated my ability to leverage Excel’s advanced functions for data cleaning, analysis, and visualization, and to interpret business insights from customer and sales data to inform strategic decisions.



**CUSTOMER PURCHASE BEHAVIOUR INSIGHTS**

High Demand for Certain Categories: Furniture and Stationery, were observed to have higher sales volumes, indicating strong customer interest. Vecta Store could benefit from focusing promotional efforts on these high-demand items or expanding the product range in these categories.

**IMPACT OF DISCOUNT & PROMOTIONS**

Increased Sales with Discounts: Furniture and Electronic products showed a higher purchase frequency, indicating that customers were more likely to buy when there were price reductions. Suggesting that discounts effectively boost volume but may reduce profitability per unit.

Opportunities for Targeted Promotions: Discounts yielded particularly high returns when applied to specific customer segments and during certain periods, like back-to-school season. Targeted discounts around high-demand times or products could maximize the impact of promotional efforts.

**SALES CHANNEL PERFORMANCE**

Instore vs. Online: The analysis showed that sales from the Instore channel constituted a relatively smaller portion than Online. Based on the analysis, the Instore percentage is 49.70% as opposed to the 50.29% for the Online channel. The higher preference for online shopping suggests that Vecta Store could invest more in enhancing the online customer experience, such as optimizing the website’s user interface or improving online promotions.

Shipping and Return Insights: Customers demonstrated higher satisfaction with certain shipping providers, leading to faster delivery and fewer returns. In the analysis, 9.83% was returned whilst 90.17% was not returned. Optimizing shipping choices by prioritizing reliable providers may enhance customer satisfaction and reduce operational costs.
